# 01-07: Thermostat weekly-program row

**Total: 35 application bytes.** Seven rows form the observed week. ACK every valid approved row with 00-07. Duplicate times are possible; no known active program-fetch command. UI option1-7 means entire week.

This example is sanitized: identities/association and illustrative calendar bytes are substituted, checksum recomputed. It is not an untouched capture.

```text
7E 2B 01 07 00 1C 10 20 30 40 A1 B2 C3 D4 03 00 00 C8 01 68 00 C8 01 68 00 C8 01 68 00 C8 01 68 01 06 25
```
