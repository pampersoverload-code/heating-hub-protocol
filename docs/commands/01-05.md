# 01-05: Thermostat presence/hourly/recontact

**Total: 22 application bytes.** Appears during recovery AND ordinary hourly operation. Respond with matching 00-05, not with a setpoint embedded in an invented extension.

This example is sanitized: identities/association and illustrative calendar bytes are substituted, checksum recomputed. It is not an untouched capture.

```text
7E 2B 01 05 00 0F 10 20 30 40 A1 B2 C3 D4 03 00 32 00 C1 00 0F 4D
```
