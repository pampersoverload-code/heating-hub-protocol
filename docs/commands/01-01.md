# 01-01: Thermostat status

**Total: 44 application bytes.** 44 bytes is the observed operating shape. Not 47 bytes of application payload. Reply with 00-01 even when its target need not change.

This example is sanitized: identities/association and illustrative calendar bytes are substituted, checksum recomputed. It is not an untouched capture.

```text
7E 2B 01 01 00 25 10 20 30 40 A1 B2 C3 D4 03 00 32 00 AD 00 01 00 00 00 FF FF FF FF FF FF B2 7B 01 2C 00 32 00 FF 00 01 01 01 01 C6
```
