# 00-03: Hub beacon-like frame

**Total: 18 application bytes.** Original periodic cadence approximately60s plus startup bursts. Exact synchronization purpose and isolated cold-start necessity remain unproven.

This example is sanitized: identities/association and illustrative calendar bytes are substituted, checksum recomputed. It is not an untouched capture.

```text
7E 2B 00 03 00 0B 00 00 00 00 10 20 30 40 00 FF 00 56
```

| Offset | Length | Field | Encoding | Confidence | Interpretation |
|---|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 0..1 | 2 | Application marker | hex | Confirmed | 7E 2B, not the RF sync word. |
| 2..3 | 2 | Kind | two raw bytes | Confirmed | Classify together with total frame length. |
| 4..5 | 2 | Payload length | BE16 | Confirmed | Total application length = this value + 7. |
| 6..9 | 4 | Unaddressed identity field | raw | Observed | 00000000; broadcast interpretation plausible. |
| 10..13 | 4 | Hub/network ID | opaque bytes | Confirmed | Matches other hub messages. |
| 14 | 1 | Slot/control | raw | Observed | 00. |
| 15..16 | 2 | Opaque control | raw | Observed | FF 00, no changing calendar identified. |
| 17 | 1 | Additive checksum | sum8 | Confirmed | Sum offsets 0..16 modulo 256. |
