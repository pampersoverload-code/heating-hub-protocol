# 00-01: Hub status/calendar/setpoint response

**Total: 35 application bytes.** Working GO_TX target: request sync +53ms. This table does not decode rare historical 31-byte variants or every unusual control-byte value.

This example is sanitized: identities/association and illustrative calendar bytes are substituted, checksum recomputed. It is not an untouched capture.

```text
7E 2B 00 01 00 1C A1 B2 C3 D4 10 20 30 40 03 FF FF FF FF 00 1A 01 02 03 04 05 00 00 FF FF FF FF FF FF 72
```

| Offset | Length | Field | Encoding | Confidence | Interpretation |
|---|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 0..1 | 2 | Application marker | hex | Confirmed | 7E 2B, not the RF sync word. |
| 2..3 | 2 | Kind | two raw bytes | Confirmed | Classify together with total frame length. |
| 4..5 | 2 | Payload length | BE16 | Confirmed | Total application length = this value + 7. |
| 6..9 | 4 | Thermostat ID | opaque bytes | Confirmed | Destination association. |
| 10..13 | 4 | Hub/network ID | opaque bytes | Confirmed | Source association. |
| 14 | 1 | Association slot | unsigned | Observed | 1..6 in this system; not a universal hardware ID. |
| 15 | 1 | Sentinel/control | raw | Observed | FF in the normal working template; rare other original values remain unresolved. |
| 16..17 | 2 | Optional absolute target | BE16 / 10 C or FFFF | Confirmed | FFFF preserves setpoint. Example 00 D7 = 21.5 C; 00 C8 = 20 C. |
| 18 | 1 | Sentinel/control | raw | Observed | FF in working normal and setpoint-change template. |
| 19 | 1 | Calendar prefix / unknown | raw | Observed | 00 in examined normal frames. |
| 20 | 1 | Year minus 2000 | binary unsigned | Confirmed | 1A = decimal26, year2026. Not BCD. |
| 21 | 1 | Month | binary unsigned | Confirmed | 1..12. |
| 22 | 1 | Day of month | binary unsigned | Confirmed | 1..31 with valid calendar combination. |
| 23 | 1 | Hour | binary unsigned | Confirmed | 0..23; reference builder uses local wall time. |
| 24 | 1 | Minute | binary unsigned | Confirmed | 0..59. |
| 25 | 1 | Second | binary unsigned | Confirmed | 0..59. |
| 26..33 | 8 | Opaque/sentinel tail | raw | Observed | 00 00 FF FF FF FF FF FF in working template. Do not assume the bytes are generally irrelevant. |
| 34 | 1 | Additive checksum | sum8 | Confirmed | Sum offsets 0..33 modulo 256. |
